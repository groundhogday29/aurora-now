Privacy Policy for Aurora Now

Effective Date: 24-02-2026

Aurora Now ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Aurora Now mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Location Information

Aurora Now may request access to your device’s location only when you choose to use the “Use My Location” feature. Location data is used solely to calculate aurora visibility estimates for your current area.

Location data is not stored on our servers and is not shared with us. It is used temporarily within the app to provide results.

Advertising Data

Aurora Now uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process data such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • Approximate location
  • App usage data

This data is collected by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Aurora Now does not have access to or control over data collected by AdMob.

Notifications

If you enable aurora alerts, the app may store your selected city and alert threshold locally on your device to provide notifications. This information is not transmitted to any external server.

Local Storage

The app may store small amounts of data locally on your device (such as alert preferences and recently viewed cities) using standard mobile storage mechanisms. This data remains on your device and is not shared externally.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to:

  • Provide aurora visibility calculations
  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Send optional aurora alerts
  • Improve app functionality

3. Data Sharing

Aurora Now does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties.

Third-party services used in the app (such as Google AdMob) may process data according to their own privacy policies.

4. User Controls

You can:

  • Deny or revoke location permission at any time via your device settings.
  • Disable aurora alerts within the app.
  • Reset your advertising ID through your device settings.

5. Children’s Privacy

Aurora Now is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
